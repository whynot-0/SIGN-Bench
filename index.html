Metric Definitions:

• Gated Acc. (Primary Metric): Joint person localization and semantic exact-match success over all benchmark items ($S = p \times A$). Shaded in light blue.

• Human Per.: Stage 1 person localization pass rate at $\mathrm{IoU} \ge 0.5$.

• Nonverbal Und. & Social Reason.: Exact-match accuracy on items passing Stage 1 localization within the corresponding capability level.
