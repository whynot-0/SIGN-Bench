Metric Definitions:

• Gated Acc. (Primary Metric): Joint localization and exact-match success over all items, $S=\dfrac{100}{N}\sum_{i\in\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{pass}}}a_i$. For $M>0$ this equals $S=100\,p\,A_{\mathrm{pass}}$. Shading marks the primary metric, not score magnitude.

• Human Per.: Stage 1 localization pass rate $p=M/N$ at $\mathrm{IoU}\ge 0.5$, with one-to-one, role-consistent matching of all required boxes.

• Nonverbal Und. & Social Reason.: Exact-match accuracy $A_{\mathrm{pass}}$ on items that pass localization, within the corresponding capability level. Multi-select items require all and only the correct options.
